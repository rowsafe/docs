---
title: What Rowsafe does for you
description: What runs by itself, what you do on a bad day, and what Rowsafe never does.
---

Once your database is set up ([Quickstart](/docs/quickstart)), most of Rowsafe runs by itself. On a bad day, you click and Rowsafe does the work.

## Every day, by itself

- **Every change is saved** to your bucket, at most about a minute behind, so you can go back to any second. [Backups and recovery](/docs/concepts/backups-and-recovery). OpenSearch, Qdrant and Meilisearch get a snapshot every 5 or 15 minutes instead.
- **Nightly backups** at 01:00 UTC: full on Sundays, only the changes other nights. Two full backups kept (about two weeks). Old ones are removed for you.
- **[Proof](/docs/concepts/restore-drills) every Sunday evening**: restores your latest backup into a scratch copy, checks it, deletes it.
- **[Pulse](/docs/guides/monitoring) every minute**: disk, connections, slow queries, cleanup, backups. A health score 0-100, findings in plain words, and **Apply fix** for most.
- **Alerts** when something needs you. Add a channel under **Settings** → **Alerts and notifications** ([channels](/docs/guides/monitoring#notification-channels)).
- **[Your weekly Pulse](/docs/guides/monitoring#your-weekly-pulse)** email every Monday.

Everything is encrypted on your server, with a passphrase only you have.

## When something goes wrong

### You deleted rows by mistake

Someone ran `DELETE FROM customers` with no `WHERE`. Production keeps running while you fix it:

1. **Rewind** → **[Find the moment](/docs/guides/find-the-moment)**, then **Rewind to just before this**.
2. **[Restore a copy](/docs/guides/restore#restore-a-copy)** as it was then, next to production.
3. **[Compare with production](/docs/guides/restore#compare-with-production)** to count missing rows per table.
4. **[Bring back](/docs/guides/restore#bring-back-rows)** the missing rows. Newer rows stay as they are.

A bad `UPDATE`? Same steps, with **Also restore rows that were changed**.

### An AI agent's migration broke the database

With [Guard](/docs/guides/ai-agents), the agent set a **Mark** right before the migration and told you its name.

- On **Marks**, click **Restore a copy at this Mark**, then compare and bring back rows.
- A dropped column or table can't be brought back as rows. [Rewind the whole database](/docs/guides/restore#rewind-the-whole-database) to the Mark instead. What was written after is kept aside for 7 days, so you can [undo](/docs/guides/restore#undo-a-rewind).

Next time, [preview the migration](/docs/guides/preview-migrations) on a copy first.

### The disk is filling up

Pulse warns up to 14 days ahead. If Rowsafe can clean up the cause (an inactive replication slot, unused indexes, tables that need cleanup), the finding has **Apply fix**. If your data simply grew, you need a bigger disk. [Disk forecast](/docs/guides/monitoring#disk-forecast).

### Backups stop working

For example, your bucket keys changed. A critical alert fires within about 10 minutes and the database stops counting as [protected](/docs/concepts/protection-status). Fix the cause, then click **Check again**.

### The server dies

- **With a [standby](/docs/guides/standby):** **Standby** → **Promote**. Automatic failover is available, off by default.
- **Without one:** your backups are safe in your bucket. Restore them onto a new server with pgBackRest: [Restore by hand](/docs/guides/restore#advanced-restore-by-hand-or-to-another-server). Practise it once.

## What Rowsafe never does

- **Never sees your data.** Backups are encrypted on your server; passphrase and keys stay there. Rowsafe stores only names, sizes, settings and metrics. [Security model](/docs/security).
- **Never restarts or changes your database on its own.** Only when an owner or admin (or their AI agent, as them) clicks and confirms, and only what the server's root allowed. Exceptions you turn on yourself: [automatic minor updates](/docs/guides/updates#minor-updates) and [automatic failover](/docs/guides/standby#automatic-failover). [Rowsafe Cloud](/docs/guides/rowsafe-cloud) servers get security updates automatically, and restarts happen in a weekly [maintenance window](/docs/guides/rowsafe-cloud#updates-and-maintenance) with an email the day before.
- **AI agents never get more than their person's rights.** An owner's or admin's agent acts right away, with a Mark first. Otherwise Rowsafe refuses and says why. Every agent change is audited. [How AI agents act](/docs/guides/agent-autonomy).
- **Never runs commands or SQL sent over the network.** The agent runs only a fixed list of tasks.
