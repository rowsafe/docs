---
title: Rowsafe Storage
description: Keep backups without a bucket of your own, encrypted so Rowsafe can't read them.
---

**Rowsafe Storage** skips the bucket setup: Rowsafe runs the bucket, and your organization gets its own private space in it. Servers Rowsafe [creates for you](/docs/guides/create-a-server) and [Rowsafe Cloud](/docs/guides/rowsafe-cloud) servers use it from the first minute.

**Your backups stay private.** They're encrypted on your server with a passphrase Rowsafe never has. We store them but can't read them, and neither can anyone who gets the bucket.

## Use Rowsafe Storage

<Steps>
<Step>

### Choose it in the installer

The installer asks where backups go:

```text
Where should backups go?
  1) Rowsafe Storage     nothing to set up (10 GB free)
  2) Your own bucket     Cloudflare R2, Amazon S3, Backblaze B2, ...
Choose 1-2 [1]:
```

Press Enter. The dashboard's command already ends with `--storage rowsafe`, which skips this question.

</Step>
<Step>

### Save the passphrase

The installer shows the passphrase once. Save it in your password manager, then type its last 4 characters. [Why it matters](/docs/guides/adopt#create-the-passphrase-and-store-it-first).

</Step>
<Step>

### That's it

The installer tests the storage and goes on to find your database. **Settings → Storage** shows your usage.

</Step>
</Steps>

Without a terminal, pass `--storage rowsafe`. The passphrase comes from `ROWSAFE_REPO_CIPHER_PASS`; without it, the installer makes one and keeps it in `/etc/rowsafe/agent.env`. Copy it to your secret manager right away: `sudo grep CIPHER_PASS /etc/rowsafe/agent.env`.

## How much is included

| Plan | Rowsafe Storage |
|---|---|
| Free | 10 GB |
| Pro | 250 GB |
| Business | 2 TB |

Usage counts full and differential backups, plus every change since the oldest kept backup. Measured hourly.

- **At 80%**: a warning in Settings → Storage, and an email to your [weekly Pulse](/docs/guides/monitoring) recipients and email alert channels.
- **At 100%**: new full backups pause. Every change is still saved, so [Rewind](/docs/guides/restore) keeps working, and **nothing is ever deleted** to make room. Upgrade, keep fewer full backups, or move to your own bucket, and full backups resume.

## How access works

Your server never holds a long-lived key. The agent gets **temporary access to your organization's space only**, valid 7 days, renewed daily. It can't see other organizations' backups. A removed server gets no new access, and its access runs out within 7 days.

If the server can't reach Rowsafe, backups go on until access runs out, 6 days after the last renewal. Rowsafe alerts you two days before. Once it reconnects, it renews by itself.

## Move to your own bucket

Your bucket's keys must never leave your server, so you move on the server:

1. [Create a bucket and an access key](/docs/guides/storage).
2. Run `curl -fsSL https://rowsafe.sh | sudo sh -s -- --setup-storage` and answer **y** to "Move them to your own bucket?". It tests the bucket and keeps your passphrase.
3. Rowsafe takes a new full backup in your bucket right away.

<Callout type="warn">
**Restores and Rewind start from the first backup in your bucket.** Older backups stay in Rowsafe Storage for 30 days, then are deleted, but Rowsafe doesn't restore from them after the move.
</Callout>

To go back, run the same command and choose Rowsafe Storage (or add `--storage rowsafe`).

## When backups are deleted

Rowsafe deletes your backups only in these cases, always after **30 days** and with an **email a week before**:

- You removed a database from Rowsafe.
- A server moved to its own bucket.
- Your organization is deleted.

Settings → Storage lists what's scheduled. To keep a removed database's backups, add it back under the same name, on a server using Rowsafe Storage, before the date. Every deletion is in the [audit log](/docs/guides/teams#audit-log).

### Kept for you

When an [AI agent](/docs/guides/agent-autonomy) removes a database, its backups are **kept** with no date, under **Kept for you** in Settings → Storage. Add the database back to use them.

Only an owner can click **Delete these backups**. Then the usual 30 days and email apply, and adding the database back still keeps them.

There's no "download your backups" button: they're encrypted with your passphrase, so a copy from Rowsafe wouldn't help. To hold them yourself, move to your own bucket.

## Good to know

- A removed database with "keep archiving" on keeps writing WAL here until you turn archiving off. Those files go with the rest after 30 days.
- Rowsafe Storage comes with Rowsafe's hosted service.
- A [second copy](/docs/guides/second-copy) always goes to your own bucket, alongside Rowsafe Storage.
- A [standby](/docs/guides/standby) isn't available yet on Rowsafe Storage: move to your own bucket first.
- Test the storage: `sudo sh install.sh --check-storage`, or `rowsafe-agent storage test` as `postgres`.
