title: Plans and limits
description: Every plan has every feature; plans differ only in how much you protect and keep.
---

| Plan | Price |
|---|---|
| Free | $0 |
| Pro | $39 per month |
| Business | $149 per month |

**No plan gates a feature.** Rewind, Proof, Pulse, Guard and the audit log are in every plan. [Pricing](/pricing).

- USD per organization, billed monthly through Polar (merchant of record). Cancel any time.
- Your own bucket is billed by its provider, not Rowsafe.

## Every limit

| | Free | Pro | Business |
|---|---|---|---|
| Servers (hosts) | 1 | 5 | 25 |
| Databases | 3 | 25 | 200 |
| Rows brought back in one [Rewind](/docs/guides/restore#bring-back-rows) run | 1 million | 10 million | 50 million |
| [Safe copies](/docs/guides/safe-copies) at once, across the organization | 1 | 5 | 20 |
| [Logs](/docs/guides/logs#busy-logs-retention-and-limits) kept for | 7 days | 30 days | 90 days |
| Log lines kept per day, across the organization | 50,000 | 2,000,000 | 10,000,000 |
| [Ask Rowsafe](/docs/guides/ask#plans-and-your-own-ai-key) built-in questions per month | none: use your own AI provider key, or your own AI through MCP | 500 | 3,000 |
| [Rowsafe Storage](/docs/guides/rowsafe-storage) | 10 GB | 250 GB | 2 TB |

- A **database** is one server instance (for PostgreSQL, a cluster) with every database inside it.
- With your own AI key, Ask Rowsafe has no monthly limit; your provider bills you.
- Rowsafe Storage is optional. Your [own bucket](/docs/guides/storage) has no Rowsafe limit.

## Rewind limits

Every plan can restore copies, compare, bring back rows and rewind a whole database. One [bring back rows](/docs/guides/restore#bring-back-rows) run is limited:

| Plan | Rows brought back in one run | Tables in one run |
|---|---|---|
| Free | up to 1 million | up to 50 |
| Pro | up to 10 million | up to 50 |
| Business | up to 50 million | up to 50 |

Over the limit, nothing changes; bring back fewer tables at a time. For now the agent also stops at 10 million rows per run, whatever the plan.

## Usage

**Settings** → **Billing** shows your plan and usage (owners manage billing there). Or run `rowsafe org`.

## Reaching a limit

Adding a host or database over the limit fails with:

```text
Your free plan allows 1 host. Upgrade to add more.
```

Lowering your plan never deletes anything. It only stops you adding more.
