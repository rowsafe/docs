Rowsafe protects the databases you already run, on your own servers or in Docker. You keep your database where it is.

- **Backs up** every change, encrypted on your server, to your own bucket.
- **Restores** to any second, in one click.
- **Proves** every week that your backups restore.
- **Watches** your database and fixes problems with one click.

## How it works

```text
You: dashboard, CLI, AI agents
        │ HTTPS
        ▼
┌─────────────────────────┐
│ Rowsafe service         │
│ schedules, alerts       │
└─────────────────────────┘
        ▲ HTTPS, outbound only
┌─────────────────────────┐
│ Your server             │
│ agent ──▶ your database │
└─────────────────────────┘
        │ encrypted backups
        ▼
Your bucket (or Rowsafe Storage)
```

1. A small open-source **agent** runs next to your database. It only makes outbound requests.
2. It encrypts backups **on your server** and sends them to **your bucket** (or [Rowsafe Storage](/docs/guides/rowsafe-storage)). Rowsafe never sees your data, keys or passphrase.
3. The **Rowsafe service** schedules the work, keeps the history and alerts you.

More: [How Rowsafe works](/docs/concepts/how-it-works).

## Supported databases

| Database | Versions | Restore to | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| PostgreSQL | 13 to 18 | Any second | [Quickstart](/docs/quickstart), [Docker](/docs/guides/docker) |
| MySQL | 8.0, 8.4 | Any second | [MySQL and MariaDB](/docs/guides/mysql) |
| MariaDB | 10.6, 10.11, 11.4 | Any second | [MySQL and MariaDB](/docs/guides/mysql) |
| MongoDB | 6.0, 7.0, 8.0 (replica sets) | Any second | [MongoDB](/docs/guides/mongodb) |
| ClickHouse | 24.8 and newer (single server) | Any second | [ClickHouse](/docs/guides/clickhouse) |
| Redis, Valkey | Redis 7.0+, Valkey 7.2+ (standalone) | Any second | [Redis and Valkey](/docs/guides/redis) |
| SQLite | 3 | Any second (WAL mode) | [SQLite](/docs/guides/sqlite) |
| OpenSearch | 2, 3 (one node, not Docker yet) | Snapshot every 5 min | [OpenSearch](/docs/guides/opensearch) |
| Qdrant | 1.13 and newer (one node) | Snapshot every 15 min | [Qdrant](/docs/guides/qdrant) |
| Meilisearch | 1.12 and newer (not Docker yet) | Snapshot every 15 min | [Meilisearch](/docs/guides/meilisearch) |

Runs on Debian 12/13 and Ubuntu 22.04/24.04, and in Docker. Each guide lists that database's limits. The short version:

- **PostgreSQL, MySQL, MariaDB, MongoDB, ClickHouse:** every feature. MongoDB skips pooling (its drivers pool).
- **Redis and Valkey:** no pooling or migration previews. Not Redis Cluster or Sentinel.
- **SQLite:** no server, so no restart, standby, pooling, server updates, logs or tuning.
- **Search engines:** snapshots, Marks, Proof, Rewind and Pulse. No standby, pooling, tuning or logs.

## Safety first

- **Never restarts your database on its own.** A restart waits for your click. ([Rowsafe Cloud](/docs/guides/rowsafe-cloud#updates-and-maintenance) servers also use a maintenance window you control.)
- **Shows what will change, in plain words,** before anything changes.
- **Proof is isolated.** The weekly restore test runs on a scratch copy and can't touch production.
- **Your secrets stay on your server.** Backups are encrypted before they leave it.
- **Signed releases.** The agent only runs releases signed by Rowsafe and rolls back a bad update.
