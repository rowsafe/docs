---
title: Backups and point-in-time recovery
description: Backups plus a continuous change log let you restore to any second.
---

A nightly dump only takes you back to last night. Rowsafe keeps two things so you can go back to any second:

1. **Backups**: a copy of the whole database, on a schedule.
2. **A change log**: every change, archived to your bucket within about a minute.

To restore, Rowsafe starts from a backup and replays the log to the second you pick. That's **Rewind**.

## Backup types

| Type | What it copies | Default schedule |
|---|---|---|
| **Full** | Everything | Sundays 01:00 UTC |
| **Differential** | What changed since the last full backup | Monday to Saturday 01:00 UTC |
| **Incremental** | What changed since the last backup of any type | On demand only |

Backups are compressed and **encrypted on your server**. Your bucket only holds ciphertext.

Run one now: `rowsafe backup app` (full) or `rowsafe backup app --type diff`.

## The recovery window

You can restore to any moment from the **start of the oldest kept full backup** to the **last archived change**. The dashboard and `rowsafe status app` show where it starts.

- **Retention** counts full backups: default 2, about two weeks. Change it (1 to 52) in the database's settings or with `rowsafe set app --retention-full 4`.
- Old backups and their change log are removed for you. **Don't add a bucket lifecycle rule that deletes objects**: it breaks restores.

## How much can you lose?

At most about **a minute** of changes, usually less.

Older setups archive every 5 minutes. **Health** shows "Backups can be up to 5 minutes behind"; **Apply fix** changes it without a restart.

If archiving stops (for example rotated bucket keys), a critical alert fires within about 10 minutes and the database is no longer [protected](/docs/concepts/protection-status).

## Restoring

On the database's **Rewind** tab, pick any second in the window or a [Mark](/docs/concepts/restore-points), then:

- **Restore a copy** next to production, compare, and **bring back** lost rows. Production keeps running.
- **Rewind the whole database**, for the worst case. Undo for 7 days.

Server lost? Restore by hand onto a new one with pgBackRest. All three: [Restore a database](/docs/guides/restore). [Proof](/docs/concepts/restore-drills) checks every week that restores work.

## By database

| | Backups | Change log | Restore to |
|---|---|---|---|
| PostgreSQL | pgBackRest: weekly full, daily differential | the WAL ([details](/docs/concepts/postgresql)) | any second, or a Mark |
| MySQL, MariaDB | XtraBackup or mariadb-backup: weekly full, daily differential | the binary log ([details](/docs/concepts/mysql)) | any second, or a Mark |
| MongoDB | `mongodump`: a full backup every day | the oplog ([details](/docs/guides/mongodb#what-rowsafe-does)) | any second, or a Mark |
| ClickHouse | ClickHouse's own `BACKUP`: weekly full, hourly differential | none | a backup (one every hour), or a Mark ([details](/docs/guides/clickhouse)) |
| Redis, Valkey | a full snapshot (RDB), sent as to a new replica | every change, from the agent attached as a replica ([details](/docs/concepts/redis)) | about a second, or exactly a Mark |
| OpenSearch | own snapshots: every 5 minutes (only what's new), a full one every day | none | a snapshot, or a Mark ([details](/docs/guides/opensearch#restores-go-back-to-a-snapshot)) |
| Qdrant | own snapshots, every 15 minutes (only changed collections) | none | a snapshot, or a Mark ([details](/docs/guides/qdrant#restores-go-back-to-a-snapshot)) |
| Meilisearch | own snapshot, every 15 minutes when something changed | none | a snapshot, or a Mark ([details](/docs/guides/meilisearch#restores-go-back-to-a-snapshot)) |

- OpenSearch, Qdrant and Meilisearch: owners can set every 5, 15 or 30 minutes, or hourly, in **Settings**.
- A whole rewind stops PostgreSQL, MySQL and MariaDB briefly. The others keep running.
