**No plan gates a feature.** Rewind (point-in-time recovery, one-click restores and Marks), Proof (weekly restore tests), Pulse (monitoring, alerts, logs, tuning and recommendations), Guard (the MCP server, the Claude Code hook, migration previews and safe copies) and the audit log are in every plan. See [pricing](/pricing).

- Prices are in USD per organization, billed monthly. Payments are handled by Polar, our merchant of record. Cancel any time.
- **Storage** in your own bucket is billed by your bucket provider, not by Rowsafe.

## Every limit

| | Free | Pro | Business |
|---|---|---|---|
| Servers (hosts) | 1 | 5 | 25 |
| Databases | 3 | 25 | 200 |
| Rows brought back in one [Rewind](/docs/guides/restore#bring-back-rows) run | 1 million | 10 million | 50 million |
| [Safe copies](/docs/guides/safe-copies) at once, across the organization | 1 | 5 | 20 |
| [Logs](/docs/guides/logs#busy-logs-retention-and-limits) kept for | 7 days | 30 days | 90 days |
| Log lines kept per day, across the organization | 50,000 | 2,000,000 | 10,000,000 |
| [Ask Rowsafe](/docs/guides/ask#plans-and-your-own-ai-key) built-in questions per month | none: use your own AI provider key, or your own AI through MCP | 500 | 3,000 |
| [Rowsafe Storage](/docs/guides/rowsafe-storage) (coming soon) | 10 GB | 250 GB | 2 TB |

- A **database** is one PostgreSQL cluster (server instance), with all the databases inside it.
- With your own AI provider key, Ask Rowsafe has no monthly question limit on any plan: your provider bills you.
- Rowsafe Storage isn't available yet. Until it is, backups go to a [bucket of your own](/docs/guides/storage), with no limit from Rowsafe.

## Rewind limits

Every plan can restore copies, compare them, bring back rows and rewind a whole database. One [bring back rows](/docs/guides/restore#bring-back-rows) run is limited:

| Plan | Rows brought back in one run | Tables in one run |
|---|---|---|
| Free | up to 1 million | up to 50 |
| Pro | up to 10 million | up to 50 |
| Business | up to 50 million | up to 50 |

Over the limit, nothing is changed. Bring back fewer tables at a time. Today the agent on your server also stops at 10 million rows in one run, whatever the plan.

## Usage

```sh
rowsafe org
```

shows your plan, limits and current usage. Owners manage billing in the dashboard under **Settings**, then **Billing**.

## Reaching a limit

Adding a host or database over your plan's limit fails with:

```text
Your free plan allows 1 host. Upgrade to add more.
```

Lowering your plan never deletes anything. It only stops you adding more until you are under the limit.
